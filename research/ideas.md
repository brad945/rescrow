# Rescrow idea catalog

Feature ideas for Rescrow, merged and deduplicated from the topic notes in [`research/notes/`](notes/). Each idea keeps the slugs of every note it came from, so the sources and the longer write-ups are one click away.

Every idea here respects the same limits:

- The app stays offline and deterministic.
- There are no AI or model calls inside the app. Anything described as "read from the agreement" means a saved answer plus a deterministic rule.
- Each idea can be built with tests in roughly 1 to 3 hours. Items marked L are over that budget and say so.
- The books keep reconciling, and money in trust leaves only through `releaseTrust(to: artist or buyer)`.

Where an idea strains one of these limits, or contradicts another idea, its Notes column says so.

**Columns.**

- **Vis** is where judges would see the idea: `shown` on screen in the demo, `rigor` in the repo and tests, or `both`.
- **Effort** is S (under 1 hour), M (1 to 3 hours) or L (over 3 hours).
- **Risk** is the risk to the recorded demo. A merged idea takes the highest risk among its sources.
- **Notes** lists the source note slugs, plus any caveat.

IDs are stable, so refer to ideas by ID in plans and commits.
